Get featured in Spain's most trusted media

túatú has been distributing press releases in Spain since 2005. Our newsroom works directly with editorial teams at El País, ABC, La Vanguardia, El Mundo, 20 Minutos, Expansión, El Confidencial and dozens of vertical publications across the country.

Unlike legacy wire services (PRWeb, EIN Presswire) that send to passive feeds, every placement we deliver is a real, indexed publication on a domain editors actually maintain. You'll receive a full PDF report with the live URL for every single placement within 72 hours of publication.

This is the same service used by international brands — including Zumba Fitness for their LATAM expansion campaigns — to land regional coverage on tight timelines.

Where exactly will my press release be published?
In Spanish regional and national outlets matching the tier you choose. You'll receive a draft media list within 24 hours of order, so you can preview the placements before we publish.
How long until my release is live?
48–72 hours after content approval, excluding weekends and Spanish public holidays. Most Standard and Pro releases are live within 48 hours.
Can I write the release in English?
Yes — our newsroom translates and editorially adapts your English draft into native Spanish at no extra cost. Translation is included with every tier.
What's the money-back guarantee?
If we don't deliver the guaranteed number of placements for your tier within 7 days of approval, you get a full refund — no questions, no clauses.
Are any topics restricted?
Crypto/blockchain, legal matters, restricted healthcare, weapons, dating services, and political content during elections require prior review. We'll flag any concerns before charging you.
