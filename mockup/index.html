Frequently asked

The basics, answered

Where will my press release be published?
In relevant local and national media outlets matching the country and tier you choose. A full media list is provided before distribution so you know exactly where your story will land.
When will my press release be published?
48–72 hours after payment and content approval, excluding weekends and public holidays. Most placements go live within 48 hours.
Can I add photos or videos?
Yes. One main image plus two contextual images are included with every release. Video coverage requires prior consultation depending on the outlet's policy.
Can I send a pre-written press release?
Yes — or our newsroom can write it for you in English, Spanish or Chinese. Translation is always included at no extra cost.
Are any topics restricted?
Some topics require pre-approval: cryptocurrency and blockchain, legal matters, restricted healthcare, weapons, dating services, and political content during election periods. We'll flag any concerns before charging you.
